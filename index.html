how it works

Three steps, no secrets shared.

Your agent reads the skill, you set the rules, and every move stays inside them.

1

Add the skill

Drop the skill file into your agent. It learns the commands and the rules in plain language.

2

Set your limits

Choose a max per trade, a daily cap and which actions are allowed. Dry-run is on by default.

3

Let it act

The agent proposes, signs through your wallet and reports back with a summary after every action.

commandwhat it does
/launchDrafts a coin from one line: name, ticker, description and art. Needs your approval before it goes live.
/buyBuys a coin up to your per-trade limit, with a slippage guard.
/sellSells part or all of a position, or follows a rule you wrote like "sell half at 2x".
/watchTracks coins and reports price, volume and holders on a schedule.
/limitsShows or changes spending caps and allowed actions.
/killStops every pending action at once and switches to dry-run.

faq
Questions


Is this live?
No. This is a draft spec and a preview. Nothing here trades or launches yet.


Can the agent drain my wallet?
By design, no. The agent only proposes actions, signing happens in your wallet, and caps apply to every action. Still, only fund what you can afford to lose.


Which agents will it work with?
Any agent that can read a plain-text skill file and call tools. Compatibility is part of the draft.


Is this financial advice?
No. Coins are volatile and you can lose everything. Agents make mistakes too.
